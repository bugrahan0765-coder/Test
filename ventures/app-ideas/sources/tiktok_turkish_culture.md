Date: 2026-11-06. Method: about 14 web searches. Language: English (founder-facing ideas, Turkish UI assumed).

## Honesty notes (read first)

I could NOT find a documented "is there an app for this" viral trend with named examples. The only hit was a marketing guide describing the format generically (see Section A).

## Candidate ideas (12)

Legend for build time: yes = 1-2 weeks solo with AI APIs; maybe; no.

### 1. Fincan Roast (working title)
- Pitch: Photo of your coffee cup becomes a funny, shareable "fal" card read by a regional-persona character; entertainment-only, group mode for friends at a table.
- Target: Turkey, women 18-40; later diaspora.
- Evidence: Category is proven by many existing apps with paid credits (links above). Demand for TikTok-native version = ASSUMPTION; no TikTok data found.
- Competitors/gap: Binnaz Abla, Falcı Bacı, Faljı, Falixir, Falzade. Gap: they are reading-centric, credit/ad-heavy. Differentiate on humour tone, a shareable image card, and a "same cup, 3 friends" group comparison, rather than on prediction accuracy.
- 3-sec hook: Cup photo, camera flips to a cartoon fortune-teller card with one savage one-liner ("Fincanında eski sevgilin hala dolanıyor").
- Monetization: free first reading, TL credit packs (low-price, e.g. few TL each), rewarded ads; EUR pack for diaspora.
- Build: yes. Vision LLM + image card renderer.
- Risk: Crowded; app store "fortune" policies; keep disclaimer "eğlence amaçlıdır", no health/money/relationship advice.

### 2. Adın Hikâyesi
- Pitch: Type a name, get a shareable card: meaning, origin, "dizi karakteri vibe", and a mini life story in a chosen tone; family naming-story prompts for parents.
- Target: Turkey (parents, couples, Gen Z); diaspora parents choosing names pronounceable in German/Dutch.
- Evidence: 889,598 births in 2025; top names Alya (girls) and Alparslan (boys); Alya's rise was tied by CNN Turk to the series Uzak Sehir (single-source commentary). https://www.ntv.com.tr/turkiye/2025-yilinda-889-bin-bebek-dunyaya-geldi-yenidoganlara-en-cok-hangi-isimler-verildi-1707949 , https://www.cnnturk.com/magazin/galeri/ekranlardan-nufus-kayitlarina-alya-2025in-en-populer-bebek-ismi-2386936 . TikTok demand for name content = ASSUMPTION.
- Competitors/gap: Many name-meaning sites/apps exist (not enumerated). Gap: shareable personality framing; diaspora pronunciation check.
- 3-sec hook: "Adının anlamı ne? Ben şoke oldum" with stylised name poster.
- Monetization: ads, TL one-off packs for printable poster; diaspora EUR printable gifts.
- Build: yes. Name dataset (public TUIK lists) + LLM; ensure meanings are accurate (source from a curated list, not free LLM output).
- Risk: LLM hallucinated etymology; thin moat; low retention.

### 3. Mani & Kına Yazarı
- Pitch: Personalised mani, türkü verses and short speeches for kına, nişan, sünnet, mezuniyet and doğum günü, with a lyric-video card to share on WhatsApp.
- Target: Turkey, brides' friends/families, 20-45; diaspora weddings.
- Evidence: ASSUMPTION. Search found no kına-specific AI app; only generic invitation/QR-album tools (Canva, Dreamina, QR album pages). https://www.canva.com/tr_tr/yapma/dugun-davetiyesi/ , https://www.qrcode-tiger.com/tr/qr-code-for-wedding-pictures
- Competitors/gap: Generic AI chat can do it but needs prompting; no ritual-aware template with rhyme meter (mani is a 7-syllable rhymed form) packaged for sharing.
- 3-sec hook: Bride's name typed, verse appears line by line over kına-night visuals with voiceover.
- Monetization: per-event packs (e.g. one-time TL fee), seasonal (wedding season), voice-over upsell.
- Build: yes. LLM with meter/rhyme constraints + TTS.
- Risk: Seasonality; LLM rhyme quality in Turkish needs testing; use only original text, not existing copyrighted songs.

### 4. Takı Defteri
- Pitch: Digital ledger for gifts received at a wedding/engagement/circumcision (who gave which takı/envelope, to return the gesture later), with QR guest entry and thank-you message generator.
- Target: Turkey, families hosting events; diaspora.
- Evidence: ASSUMPTION based on well-known custom; one Milliyet gallery piece references changes in takı ceremony but I did not read it (https://www.milliyet.com.tr/galeri/son-dakika-dugunlerdeki-taki-toreninde-devrim-gibi-degisiklik-6543420/4). No competitor-gap data.
- Competitors/gap: Paper notebooks, Excel, WhatsApp notes. Gap: nobody (found) packages it culturally. Record-keeping only, no financial advice.
- 3-sec hook: Messy notebook with names next to a clean app list: "Düğünden sonra kim ne takmıştı?"
- Monetization: one-off TL pack per event, PDF export, thank-you message credits.
- Build: yes. Simple CRUD app; local-first storage helps privacy.
- Risk: Narrow, infrequent use; privacy of gift data; weak virality unless content is sketch-style.

### 5. Dizi Sahnesi
- Pitch: Describe a mundane situation ("annem aradı, açmadım") and get it rewritten as a dramatic Turkish-series scene: script, dramatic music-style voice, subtitle card. Archetype characters only, no real actors or faces.
- Target: Turkey Gen Z, plus global fans of Turkish series (Balkans, Central Asia, LatAm audiences per academic source below).
- Evidence: TikTok has a dedicated Turkish-series hub and fan campaigns (links above). Turkish series audience is international: https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/4250551 (as summarised by search; verify). Format demand for AI scene remixing = ASSUMPTION.
- Competitors/gap: Generic meme/AI video apps; none Turkish-drama-specific found (not exhaustively checked).
- 3-sec hook: Close-up text: "Çay demlenmedi" with slow-zoom, dramatic string stab and subtitled monologue.
- Monetization: free watermark clips, TL/credit packs for no-watermark and voices; international users pay in USD.
- Build: maybe. LLM script + TTS + simple video render; polish of video is the work.
- Risk: Copyright if copying real series lines or music; do not mimic real actors' voices/likenesses; trend may fade.

### 6. Şive Atlası
- Pitch: Type a sentence, hear and see it in a chosen regional Turkish accent/idiom (Karadeniz, Ege, Kayseri, Istanbul "kanka", etc.), so users can send it to friends.
- Target: Turkey, 16-35.
- Evidence: ASSUMPTION. Regional-accent humour is a known TikTok genre but I found no source quantifying it.
- Competitors/gap: Comedians do it manually; no app found (not exhaustively checked).
- 3-sec hook: "Bunu Karadenizli söylese?" with text appearing in dialect and voice.
- Monetization: ads, TL packs for extra regions, voice packs.
- Build: maybe. Dialect quality of LLM and TTS is the main dependency; TTS accents are hard.
- Risk: HIGH on your hard constraint. Regional humour can slide into mocking ethnic/regional groups. Mitigation: affectionate "my own hometown" framing, no ethnic-group stereotypes, exclude minority languages, curated phrases rather than free generation. If you cannot guarantee that, drop this idea.

### 7. Yanlış Defteri
- Pitch: Photograph a question you got wrong, app files it into a subject-tagged mistake notebook, writes a short explanation and re-quizzes you with similar questions before the exam.
- Target: Turkey, YKS/TYT/AYT and LGS students (and KPSS).
- Evidence: Exam pressure is documented (academic studies on YKS/LGS anxiety and rankings focus): https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/download/article-file/5694029 . "Yanlış defteri" as a student practice = ASSUMPTION from general knowledge (not sourced in my searches). Webtekno lists YKS apps such as Kunduz: https://www.webtekno.com/yks-mobil-uygulamalar-android-ios-h106549.html
- Competitors/gap: Kunduz (question solving), generic flashcard apps. Gap: the mistake-notebook workflow with spaced repetition and automatic topic tagging.
- 3-sec hook: Messy paper yanlış defteri vs one photo that organises into a topic heatmap ("En çok yanlış yaptığın konu: Üslü sayılar").
- Monetization: students have low budget; weekly/monthly TL subscription, parent-paid; free tier with limited scans.
- Build: maybe. Vision OCR on handwritten/math questions is error-prone; explanations could be wrong.
- Risk: Accuracy (wrong explanation harms trust); school-year seasonality; ensure no "medical" claims regarding stress.

### 8. Bayram & Ramazan Kartı
- Pitch: Generate personalised bayram/Ramazan greetings (per recipient: dede, anne, patron, eski arkadaş) as card + voice note, in tone and dialect, ready for WhatsApp.
- Target: Turkey broadly; diaspora strongly (sending to family at home).
- Evidence: Imsakiye apps are plentiful (1.1M installs on one ad-supported app per aggregator), i.e. seasonal app demand is real, but greeting-card demand is ASSUMPTION. https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.erbasaran.ramazanimsakiyesi , https://www.haberturk.com/ramazan/imsakiye . No TikTok trend found.
- Competitors/gap: imsakiye apps and generic greeting-card makers. Gap: recipient-aware personalised text plus voice.
- 3-sec hook: "Teyzene bayram mesajı atmayı unuttun mu?" 10-second generation.
- Monetization: ads, small TL packs; diaspora EUR upsell for voice cards.
- Build: yes. LLM + TTS + card renderer.
- Risk: Extreme seasonality (build for early 2027 Ramadan, verify dates); religious sensitivity: keep respectful, no jokes about religion; low retention.

### 9. Mektup Çözücü (diaspora)
- Pitch: Photograph a German/Dutch official letter and get a plain-language Turkish summary: who sent it, what it asks, deadline, suggested next step (translation/summary only, not legal advice).
- Target: Turkish diaspora in Germany/Netherlands, esp. older generation and new arrivals.
- Evidence: ASSUMPTION. Searches show diaspora content on TikTok revolves around life comparisons and cost-of-living, and academic work notes cultural-adjustment challenges; no direct app demand source. https://onedio.com/haber/almanya-da-yasayan-gurbetcinin-eski-turkiye-yi-anlattigi-videosu-saskina-cevirdi-1332743 , https://acikbilim.yok.gov.tr/handle/20.500.12812/168698
- Competitors/gap: Google Translate/Lens, general chatbots. Gap: Turkish-first UX, structured "action + deadline" output, template replies.
- 3-sec hook: Scary German letter on screen, camera scan, "Bu mektup senden 14 gün içinde cevap istiyor".
- Monetization: EUR pricing, subscription or per-scan packs; diaspora has far higher willingness to pay than TL market.
- Build: maybe. Vision/OCR + LLM; document privacy handling.
- Risk: Legal/accuracy liability (do not give legal advice; strong disclaimers); GDPR for sensitive letters; German TikTok ban debate is mentioned in a tech article (https://shiftdelete.net/?p=1003938), a distribution risk worth watching.

### 10. Nine Tarifi
- Pitch: Record your grandmother or mother cooking and talking ("bir tutam, göz kararı"); the app turns the voice into a structured recipe card with estimated grams/minutes, keeps the audio, and builds a family recipe book.
- Target: Turkey and diaspora (strong emotional pull for people living abroad).
- Evidence: ASSUMPTION; emotional/"real connection" angle matches TikTok Next 2026 signals (links in A1). Not validated by app-market data in my searches.
- Competitors/gap: Large Turkish recipe sites/apps (Nefis Yemek Tarifleri, Yemek.com; not researched here) and generic recipe savers. Gap: oral, imprecise family recipes captured in the original voice.
- 3-sec hook: Grandma says "bu kadar un koy" with hands; screen shows card "~2.5 su bardağı un" with her audio.
- Monetization: free for 3 recipes, then yearly pack; printable recipe book (physical gift) upsell, EUR for diaspora.
- Build: yes. Whisper-class speech-to-text for Turkish + LLM structuring; quantity estimation needs a "check me" edit step.
- Risk: Quantity guesses may be wrong (label as estimates); slow organic repeat use; low frequency product.

### 11. Tezahürat Atölyesi
- Pitch: Enter a player/team nickname and get original chant lyrics with a clap/rhythm pattern for the stands, plus a share card.
- Target: Turkey football fans (no betting content).
- Evidence: ASSUMPTION. Sports-fan clips go viral (the "six seven" meme started from a basketball fan video: https://tr.euronews.com/2025/12/17/2025e-damga-vuran-en-tuhaf-10-kulturel-trend ) but no data on chant-generator demand.
- Competitors/gap: Fan forums, generic chat AI. Gap: rhythm-aware chant structure with fan-card output.
- 3-sec hook: "Yeni transfere tezahürat yazdım" with lyric-by-lyric karaoke beat.
- Monetization: ads, TL packs; sponsor potential later.
- Build: yes. LLM with syllable-count constraints.
- Risk: Trademark/club names; rivalry abuse and insults, profanity moderation required; ethnic/religious slurs in chants must be blocked; seasonal with the football calendar.

### 12. Hatıra Defteri 2.0
- Pitch: A class/friend-group digital autograph book (hatıra defteri/yıllık): everyone writes via link, AI produces a "sınıf rolleri" page and a printable keepsake.
- Target: Turkey, students graduating (lise, üniversite), 15-25.
- Evidence: ASSUMPTION. Based on the known tradition; no sourced data. Related: "Anılar Bulutta" for wedding/engagement albums exists (https://apps.apple.com/app/id6587549998), which shows keepsake apps exist but not student-focused.
- Competitors/gap: Instagram/WhatsApp groups, yearbook print services. Gap: ritual-native format plus fun AI roles.
- 3-sec hook: Real defter with scribbles flipping to the phone version: "Mezuniyet için son 7 gün".
- Monetization: per-class pack paid by one organiser (TL); print upsell.
- Build: yes. Link-based form + generation + PDF.
- Risk: Seasonal (May-June); minors' data (child-safety/KVKK issues); bullying in "roles" feature, so moderation needed.
