# D2 in-sample results: month-end USD flows into the London 4pm fix (2015-2020, cost model v2)

Spec: research/hypotheses/D2_fx_month_end_fix.md. Last trading day of the month, direction = sign of the US500 month-to-date return at entry (sell USD if up), exit 16:01 London, stop 3 x ex-ante expected std. Months lost: HAR warm-up (first ~5 months of 2015) and months where US500 prices are unavailable (excluded months and the month after them, since the base is the previous month-end close).
